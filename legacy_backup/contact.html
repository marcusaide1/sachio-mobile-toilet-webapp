❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to deploy an expandable container house?

Expandable modular container homes arrive fully assembled with pre-installed plumbing, flooring, and electricals. Expanding the 2 side rooms takes 4 people approximately 15 minutes using hydraulic or manual pull mechanisms.

Are the HDPE portable toilets resistant to extreme weather and sun?

Yes. All TOPINDUS portable toilets are rotomolded from 100% Virgin High-Density Polyethylene infused with UV-8 stabilizers. They do not fade, crack, or degrade in tropical heat or sub-zero climates.

What is the warranty and lifespan?

All mobile toilet cabins include a 3-year structural guarantee with a 15+ year operational lifespan. Container houses carry a 25-year structural lifespan guarantee.
